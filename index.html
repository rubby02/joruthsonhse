What We Offer

Our Services

A comprehensive suite of safety services for individuals, corporates and industries across Nigeria.

HSE Training

Comprehensive Health, Safety & Environment training covering regulations, risk management and safe working practices at Levels 1, 2 and 3.

Fire Extinguisher Servicing

Professional inspection, maintenance and recharging of all extinguisher types ensuring your equipment is always operationally ready.

Risk Assessment & Hazard Control

Systematic workplace risk assessments that identify hazards and implement effective control measures to minimise incidents and liability.

Industrial Safety Consultancy

Expert advisory services helping organisations build robust safety management systems aligned with Nigerian and international standards.

Environmental Management

End-to-end environmental management services that help businesses achieve compliance and reduce their ecological footprint.

Safety Equipment Sales

Quality PPE supply — fire extinguishers, safety boots, helmets, gloves, reflective jackets, respirators, harnesses and eye goggles.

Our Curriculum

Training Programs

Industry-recognised courses designed to advance safety careers and protect your workforce.

01
HSE Level 1, 2 & 3 Certification

Nationally and internationally recognised certificates from foundation through to professional management level.

02
Workplace Safety Education

Practical programmes covering safety regulations, hazard identification and safe working procedures for all industries.

03
First Aid & CPR Training

Certified hands-on first aid and CPR training equipping participants to respond effectively in workplace emergencies.

04
Fire Marshal Training

Comprehensive fire warden certification covering prevention, evacuation coordination and extinguisher use.

05
Construction Safety Training

Site-specific safety training covering scaffolding, PPE selection, working at height and construction hazard management.

06
Oil & Gas Safety Courses

Specialised programmes aligned with upstream, midstream and downstream oil & gas industry safety requirements.

07
Fire Safety & Emergency Response

Integrated emergency response planning — fire control, evacuation coordination and crisis communication strategies.

08
Environmental Impact Assessment

Training on conducting EIAs and developing mitigation plans to comply with Nigerian environmental regulations.
